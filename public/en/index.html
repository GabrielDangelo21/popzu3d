About popzu

A small studio in Denderleeuw.

We print our figures in colour on our own 3D printers, with a texture that looks like knitting. They are sturdy printed objects, not fabric plush toys. We work with original designs by Whale 3D Studio and hold a commercial licence to print and sell them.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do the photos show my own figure?

No. They are example photos of the design, sometimes in a styled scene. Your figure is printed just for you after you order. Colours and details may vary slightly.

Is it a plush toy?

No. The knitted look is a texture in the printed plastic. It's a sturdy figure to put on display, not a soft toy. It's a decorative object and not suitable for children under 3, as it contains small parts.

How long until my figure is ready?

Every figure is printed and assembled after you order. Allow 1 working day, plus delivery time if you have it shipped.

How big is a figure?

Every figure comes in two sizes: small (9–16 cm) and large (17–32 cm). The exact height is shown for each figure. A different size is possible on request: send us a message on WhatsApp.

How do I order and pay?

Tap ‘Order via WhatsApp’ on the figure you like. Your message is ready to send, with the name and the price. We confirm your order, the total price and the payment method on WhatsApp.

How do I get my figure and what does shipping cost?

You can pick up your figure for free in Denderleeuw or have it shipped. The delivery times below start once your figure is ready and sent.

Keep it out of direct sunlight and away from heat sources. A soft brush is the best way to dust it.
